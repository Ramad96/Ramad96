# Amanah Digital

### [amanahdigital.co.uk](https://amanahdigital.co.uk)

**Where Innovation Serves Iman.**

Amanah Digital is an Islamic digital studio dedicated to creating thoughtful, accessible tools that nurture iman and serve the Ummah.

The aim is simple: use technology with purpose — removing friction from beneficial actions, encouraging consistency in worship and creating digital experiences that help Muslims engage with their deen.

## Amanah Digital Products

### [Amanah Digital](https://amanahdigital.co.uk)
The home of Amanah Digital and the growing collection of tools, apps and experiments built under the brand.

### [Sadaqah Jariyah Radio](https://radio.amanahdigital.co.uk)
A Quranic audio platform designed to make it easy to dedicate ongoing recitation as sadaqah jariyah for someone who has passed away.

### [Taraaweeh Counter](https://taraaweeh.amanahdigital.co.uk)
A distraction-free Taraaweeh tracker for keeping count of rakaat while remaining focused on the prayer, with an accompanying Apple Watch app.

### [Islamic Quiz](https://quiz.amanahdigital.co.uk)
An interactive browser-based Islamic quiz for testing and expanding your knowledge while competing with friends and family.

### [Khatam Together](https://quran.amanahdigital.co.uk)
A collaborative Quran completion tool that lets family and friends divide a Khatam or 40 Yaseen between themselves and track their collective progress.

### [Noor — Daily Ibadah Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/noor-daily-ibadah-tracker/id6760976099)
An iPhone companion for goal-orientated spiritual growth across Salah, Quran, Dhikr, fasting and Sadaqah.

### [Pebble — Dhikr Tracker](https://github.com/Ramad96/Pebble)
A lightweight macOS menu bar companion for quietly tracking dhikr throughout the working day.

### [Sabah — Quranic Alarm Clock](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sabah/id6763389763)
An iPhone alarm clock that lets you wake up to Quranic verses and begin the day with the words of Allah.

### [Qibla](https://qibla.amanahdigital.co.uk)
A location-aware Qibla compass that shows your real-time direction towards the Kaabah in Makkah.

### [Muhasabah — Daily Reflection](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/muhasabah/id6778949153)
A private space for daily spiritual reflection, accountability and intentional self-review.

### [Amal — A Home for Your Duas](https://amal.amanahdigital.co.uk)
A shared space for keeping your duas, inviting people you care about and building a living collection of hope and remembrance.
